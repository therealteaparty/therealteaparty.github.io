ABOUT ME

My name is Tea! My work explores narrative and functional topics in the new developmental age of technology, with an emphasis in the cross section between new technology and art practices. I have a focus in creating virtual worlds which may develop a relationship with users in the form of interactive games or visual experiences. These projects, built from emerging tools and softwares, aim to suggest new ways of learning and experiencing our metaphysical world through innovative experiences.

I come from a background in the performing arts, debate, and digital illustration. I love to join in on game jams, create personal digital and traditional art pieces, and (of course) play games. I have a special interest in story-driven singleplayer video games, as well as free-to-play multiplayer combat games. My interests in media, philosophy, people, and lifestyles find their way through concept art and paintings, which you may see below.
